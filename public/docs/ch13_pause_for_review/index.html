Conclusion | 結論

Although causal guidelines discussed in this chapter are often referred to as criteria, this term does not seem entirely appropriate. Although it may be a desirable goal to place causal inferences on a irm quantitative and structural foundation, at present we generally do not have all the information needed for doing so.
